Deforestation Crisis: Facts & Figures

Global Deforestation

  • 18.7 million acres lost annually (FAO 2020)
  • 420 million hectares destroyed since 1990
  • Forests absorb 2.6 billion tonnes of CO₂ yearly
  • Tropical forests disappearing at 10 football fields per minute

India's Forests

  • Forest cover: 24.62% of land (ISFR 2023)
  • Lost 1.88 lakh hectares in last 5 years
  • 668 districts affected by deforestation
  • Illegal logging & mining major threats in Northeast & Western Ghats

Consequences

  • Loss of 70% wildlife habitat
  • Increased human-wildlife conflict
  • 15 billion trees cut yearly worldwide
  • Contributes 12-15% of global GHG emissions
